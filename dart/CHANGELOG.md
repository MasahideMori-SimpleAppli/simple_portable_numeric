## 0.1.0

* Added `PortableDecimal.parseLenient` (exponent notation such as `"1.2E-05"`, `".5"`, `"1."`).
* Added `pow`, `isInteger` and `toBigInt` to both types, and `stripTrailingZeros` to `PortableDecimal`.
* Added `PortableRational.toDecimalExact`.
* Added the rounding mode `EnumRoundingMode.up`.
* Spec version 2. Existing results do not change.
